# Style: extreme-energy (detail, written from the demo build t153, 2026-11-14)

## 0. Read this first: niche, reading order, status

**Niche (two tiers; do not mix them).**
- **Tier 2 - general fit of the style (REFERENCE ONLY).** Observed in this build: event hype and sports-festival teasers (skate, BMX, wakeboard, motorsport-like action), sponsorship or venue reels, "coming to your city" announcements, one-big-number stamps (height, speed, record). Works with 2 to 4 beats in 10 to 15 s. Not for slow explainers, many equal numbers, dense UI, enforcement or sad stories. Tier 2 never widens tier 1.

**Status.** DEMO-ONLY build: one silent 12.0 s sample (`samples/sample-t153.mp4`, topic shape t153, Jev fit 0.66). **Voice-over, word timing, captions and music were NOT exercised**; the caption and sound sections below are carried over from the profile and are untested. Everything else was measured on this build.

**Example video shows the recipe, not the niche.** The content on screen is generic and labelled "Minh hoạ" (no real company, price or spec).

Profile: `skill/references/styles/profiles/extreme-energy.yaml` (energy 5, dark mode). Format 1080 x 1920, 30 fps, 360 frames, 7.2 MB.
Marks: **SUBST** = a named font is not installed and a local one replaces it. **DEVIATION** = knowingly differs from the profile.
- SUBST 1: Anton (condensed) is not available; see section 2.
- DEVIATION 1: the profile says "often cropped by frame edges". The acceptance rule forbids text cut by the frame on a held frame, so no word is cropped; the size is chosen to fill 85-95% of the width instead.
- DEVIATION 2: no footage; stills generated by agy with scale push (profile allows this).

## 1. Tokens

```css
:root{--bg:#0a0c1f; --ink:#fff; --red:#e22a36; --gold:#f5a623; --navy:#2a2352; --mute:rgba(255,255,255,.6)}
```
Contrast, WCAG formula computed by hand (no script could run in this sandbox; values are rounded):

| Pair | Ratio | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ink on bg | 19.4:1 | all big type |
| gold on bg | 9.6:1 | stat, accent word, kicker |
| bg on gold | 9.6:1 | text on gold bar and on the "Minh hoạ" chip |
| ink on navy | 14.4:1 | type on the navy gradient |
| gold on navy | 7.1:1 | ok for text |
| mute on bg (over-bg result about #9da0a5) | 7.3:1 | footer line |
| ink on red | 4.55:1 | ok only for text >= 24 px bold; all red-bar text here is 110-170 px |
| red on bg | 4.26:1 | FILL ONLY (bars, stamps, ring); never small text |
| bg on ink (white bar) | 19.4:1 | text on the white row |

Rule: red is a fill, never thin text. Text on photos always sits on a gradient scrim that reaches rgba .9 under the text.

## 4. Layers, back to front

1. `#root` bg. 2. `#stage` (shaken on impacts) holding one `section.sc` per beat, only one visible. Each beat: photo (oversize 1200 x 2120, offset -60/-100 so shake and push never show an edge) then a CSS gradient scrim, then type. 3. `#streak` (baked PNG, screen blend). 4. `#wipe` red skewed bar (z 55). 5. `#chip` and `#foot` (z 50). 6. `#grain` baked PNG 7% overlay (z 45). 7. `#flash` white (z 60).
Grain and streak were baked with ffmpeg `noise` plus `avgblur`; no live CSS blur or radial gradient is used.

## 5. Components

- **Skewed bar** `.bar`: solid red / gold / white, `skewX(-8deg)`, text inside stays upright.
- **Row** (SKATE / BMX / WAKEBOARD): bar + giant word + italic label bottom-left + number top-right (48 px). Rows alternate red, gold, white and enter from alternating sides.
- **Stat stamp**: gold number 400 px, red underline bar 20 px, label below.
- **Trajectory + apex ring**: white 8 px stroke drawn by dashoffset, gold 8 px ring (80 px) at the apex.
- **Chip** "MINH HOẠ": gold, persistent from 0.05 s to the end.

## 6. Motion language

- Slam: from scale 1.6 and alpha 0 to 1 in 0.15-0.18 s `power4.in` (accelerates into the hit), then a 0.14 s squash (1.06 / 0.94) `power2.out`. Decaying seeded shake: 8 steps of 0.05 s, amplitude 8-18 px, only on kicks and drops.
- Flash 0.8 (hook slam), 0.8 (beat change), 0.7 (apex), 0.5 (end card), each 0.16-0.25 s.
- Speed ramp on a still: hook 1.0 -> 1.16 over 3.1 s `power3.in`; apex 1.0 -> 1.14 in 1.1 s `power4.in`, then 1.14 -> 1.17 `sine.out` (near-hold).
- Counter: gsap tween on a plain object with onUpdate, `power3.out`, 0 -> 4.5 in 0.9 s, ends 0.05 s before the stamp so it is never held mid-count.
- Hard entrances: sides `expo.out` 0.2-0.35 s from +-700..1300 px.
- Micro-motion in holds: rows drift 14 px, headline scale 1.04, apex ring pulse, image drift, so no frame is dead.
- Transitions: only two languages. Red skewed bar wipe (0.3 s, linear from x -1400 to 2380, scene swap at its centre) and white flash punch. Hook -> disciplines and stat -> end card use the wipe; disciplines -> stat uses the flash.

Red bar wipe and flash punch only. Whip pan with streak was prepared (`streak.png`) but a bar wipe read cleaner on 9:16; keep one language per cut.

## 10. Image guidance for agy
Two stills made (about 60 s each, 0.9-1.0 MB): "silhouette of a faceless skateboarder frozen at the apex above a concrete bowl at dusk, navy-black sky, red and gold rim light, floodlights, grainy poster, empty dark space at the top". Keep the top third empty (headline zone) and the subject in the lower-middle for the hook, upper-right for the apex beat. agy drew a cream paper border on the skate image: the oversize crop (offset -60/-100) hides it. Faceless silhouettes avoid real people.

## 11. Safe handling of facts
Generic content only. Number 4,5 M is invented, so the label says "SỐ LIỆU MINH HOẠ" and the "Minh hoạ" chip plus footer stay on every frame. Austin is the only real name (from the topic shape); no event name, date, price or brand.

## 12. Must not
- No cropped text on a held frame; no red thin text; no Segoe Print.
- No shake or flash except on kicks and drops; no third transition language.
- No real brand cans, logos, events; no real athletes.
- No fake data without the "Minh hoạ" label.

## 13. Traps seen in real builds (this build, measured)

1. **Fromto leak.** `fromTo(..., immediateRender:false)` leaves the element at its CSS position before its start time. First render showed AUSTIN and BÙNG NỔ already at 0.1 s and all three rows during the wipe at 3.27 s. Fix: `gsap.set(sel,{autoAlpha:0})` for every entrance target and `tl.set(sel,{autoAlpha:1}, t)` at its time.
2. **Hero word wider than the frame.** AUSTIN at 350 px with scaleX .8 measured about 1176 px (> 1080) and was cut at the right edge; 290 px fits (about 970 px). A 9-letter word at 200 px was cut too; 140 px fits.
3. **Label under giant text.** Row label (34 px) at bottom-left sat under the 240 px word and "XE ĐẠP Đ" was hidden. Rows grew from 300 to 340 px and the word dropped to 210 px; label bottom 12 px.
4. **Stat underline through the comma.** Red bar at y +360 cut the comma of "4,5 M"; moved to +450.
5. **Scrim too thin for gold on a photo.** Gradient reached only .18 alpha at the stat; changed so alpha is .9 from 56% of the height down.
6. **Early empty panel.** Rows at 3.65 left 0.5 s of header on bare navy. Rows moved to 3.4 / 3.7 / 4.0 (empty time now about 0.2 s).
7. **CSS skew on a GSAP-tweened x.** lint error `gsap_css_transform_conflict` on `#wipe`: set `skewX` with `gsap.set` instead of CSS.
8. **Wipe width.** A 1500 px skewed bar never covers 1080 px plus the skew offset (about 300 px each side); 1900 px does, and the covered window is x 380-600, so the swap sits at x about 490 (path -1400 -> 2380, linear).
9. **Duplicate image.** Using `skate.jpg` twice gave lint `duplicate_media_discovery_risk`; a copy `skate2.jpg` clears it.
10. **Inspect false positives.** `text_occluded` under `#wipe` at 3.33 s is intentional (`data-layout-allow-occlusion`); `content_overlap` on scaled spans comes from the unscaled layout box of the scaleX .8 wrapper. `validate` printed 1:1 contrast for the skewed chip (5 warnings at 1.2, 3.6, 6, 8.4, 10.8 s); the rendered chip is bg on gold, 9.6:1.
11. **Tools.** `frame_check.py` needs numpy and could not run here; checks were done with ffmpeg contact sheets every 0.25 s plus `inspect`. Fix the environment before relying on frame_check.
12. **Counter in transit.** The count 0.9 -> 4,4 M is visible during 6.55-7.45; it is not held: the final value lands at 7.5 s and nothing else reads "mid-count".
