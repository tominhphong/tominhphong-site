# Style: expressive-material (detail, written from the t118 demo build, 2026-11-14)

## 0. Read this first: niche, reading order, status

**Niche (two tiers; do not mix them).**
- **Tier 2 - general fit of the style (REFERENCE ONLY), as observed in this build.** Personal, consumer-device and mobile-UI stories where "the product adapts to you": phone/OS updates that personalise colour, widgets, themes; app feature tours; consumer launches with a settings-and-toggles flavour. Jev fit for the shape "Phone operating system update personalises colours and widgets" was 0.84 and the build confirmed it: every beat (seed reseed, shape morph, container transform) maps to a real thing that happens in such an update. Not for: dense numbers (no counter recipe was exercised), enforcement or sad stories, code/terminal/spec stories (profile `avoid`), data dashboards.

**Status.** DEMO-ONLY build: ONE silent 12.0 s sample (1080x1920, 30 fps), no voice-over, no VieNeu, no word timing, no music, no captions. **Voice, caption, sound and word-anchor rules were NOT exercised**; sections 8 (captions) and 9 (sound) below are copied from the profile and marked UNTESTED. Content is generic and labelled "Minh hoạ" on screen; no real brand, product, price or spec.

**Marks.** MEASURED = read from this build's files or tool output. HAND = computed by hand (WCAG formula, about +-0.1). ASSUMED = invented here, not tested beyond the sample. SUBST = a local font replaces the profile's font.

Profile: `skill/references/styles/profiles/expressive-material.yaml` (energy 3, mixed palette, family brand-inspired). Character: soft, bold, personal; one seed colour tints everything; big rounded shapes morph; containers expand into screens.

DEVIATION 1: the profile says "layout: containers fill 60-80% of the frame". In 1080x1920 the screen container is 936 x 1140 (47% of the frame area, 59% of the height) because a 2-line headline sits above it. ASSUMED.
DEVIATION 2: profile display weight 700 width 125 and hero swell to 800: SegUIVar has no width axis used and no verified 800 (see traps). Swell is 400 -> 700.

## 1. Tokens (one seed -> tonal set; three seeds used)

Every colour is a tone of the scene seed; a reseed tweens ALL tokens at once. Tokens: `bg` page, `scr` screen surface, `pc` primary container (clock, selection pill, kicker), `sc` secondary container (weather, music, card), `tc` tertiary container (battery, sheet), `acc` accent, `ink`, `mute`.

|---|---|---|---|
| bg | #fcf7fc | #f3fbfa | #fff8f4 |
| scr | #f4ebf7 | #e6f3f2 | #fbeee3 |
| pc | #f0dcfa | #cdeceb | #ffdcbe |
| sc | #eedff5 | #d9e8e7 | #f5e0cd |
| tc | #ffdad8 | #d6e3f6 | #e9eac0 |

| ink | #201c22 | #191c1c | #201b16 |
| mute | #4c4650 | #3f4948 | #51453a |

Purple row is the profile's own (bg, ink, mute, accent, primary_container, secondary_container, tertiary_container). Teal and orange seeds are the profile's examples (#006a6a, #8b5000); their tonal steps are ASSUMED (hand-picked, not generated by an HCT tool).

Contrast (HAND, WCAG 2.x): ink on bg 15.9:1 (purple); acc on bg 5.65 (purple), 6.1 (orange), about 6.1 (teal); acc on pc 4.65 (purple, tightest pair, used only for the 100 px hero word and never for small text); white on acc 5.98 (purple). `hyperframes validate` MEASURED: "70 text elements pass WCAG AA" with all three seeds sampled. Rule: small text uses `ink` or `mute` only; `acc` text only at 100 px (hero word) or on `bg`. Toggle-off knob and 4 px outline use `mute`.

Reseed implementation (MEASURED works): one proxy `{k:0->1}` per reseed, `onUpdate` writes `--token` as an RGB lerp between two fixed seed rows; `fromTo` with `immediateRender:false`. Do NOT tween the CSS variables directly with GSAP (hex strings are not interpolated reliably; ASSUMED, not tested).

## 5. Layers, back to front

1. `#root` bg (var --bg). 2. `#deco` morphing blob (400 px, top-right, rotates 0 -> 50 deg over 12 s, pulses 1.05 on beats; fastest-moving background element). 3. kicker, headline(s). 4. `#screen` (scale 0.96 -> 1 in 0.7 s `expo.out`, then 1.00 -> 1.02 linear over the rest = micro-motion in every hold). 5. widgets, FAB. 6. `#sheet` (container transform target). 7. `#card` (second container transform target). 8. footer. No fade layer: frame 0 already shows the finished headline and screen.

## 6. Components

- **Morphing shape** `sp(P)`: 96-point polygon, radius = superellipse `1/((|cos|/ax)^n + (|sin|/ay)^n)^(1/n)` times `1 + amp*cos(8 theta)`. The shape is defined by four numbers, so any two shapes have identical point counts and morph by tweening the numbers (no pre-authored path files): squircle `{220,220,4,0}`, scallop `{243,243,2,.07}`, pill `{255,150,4,0}`, circle `{245,245,2,0}`. All must stay inside the 540 viewBox: a squircle with ax 250 reaches 297 at the corner and is cut off.
- **Widget** flat tonal fill, no border, no shadow, radius 40.
- **FAB** 120 px accent square radius 40 with a white plus (inline SVG, 6 px round stroke).
- **Sheet** same rect as the FAB at t 3.6, grows to 864 x 484 at (36,620) radius 48, fill `tc`.
- **Swatch** 170 px circle in the three fixed seed accents (these do not retint), 36 px label under; selection ring 206 px, 8 px `ink` border, slides between swatches with `back.out(1.4)`.
- **Selection pill** `pc` rounded rect behind chip labels, slides x 0 -> 294 -> 588.
- **Toggle** 150 x 84, off = `scr` with 4 px `mute` outline; on = `acc` fill + white knob that travels 70 px with `back.out(2)`.
- **Row** 840 x 160 radius 40 `pc`: 96 px scallop icon (accent), 42 px label, toggle.
- **Preview tile** 264 x 190 radius 40 with a 140 px accent shape (squircle, circle, scallop).
- **Kicker pill** `pc`, 32 px 600, text "Minh hoạ".
- No faces, no shadows, no borders except the toggle outline.

## 7. Motion language (MEASURED timings)

- Entrance: `back.out(1.3)`, 0.55 s, y 30 -> 0, scale 0.9 -> 1, stagger 0.06 s with `power1.in` ease, starting 0.05 s. Order clock, weather, battery, music, chips, FAB.
- Hero word weight swell 400 -> 700 over 1.1 s `power3.out` starting 0.2 s (second headline: 1.0 s from 8.2 s).
- Spring slider: thumb x 0 -> 320 and fill 200 -> 520 px in 0.7 s `back.out(1.6)` at 1.7 s.
- **Container transform:** FAB press 0.9 (0.1 s) -> cross-fade to sheet (0.12 s) -> sheet rect grows 0.75 s `power3.inOut` (left, top, width, height, borderRadius tweened); content fades in 0.35 s at 0.45 s into the growth (4.05 s); swatches pop 0.6 -> 1 `back.out(1.6)` stagger 0.06. Reverse (collapse) 0.5 s, content out first (0.15 s).
- **Reseed:** 0.55 s `power2.inOut`, at 4.95 s (purple -> teal) and 6.55 s (teal -> orange); ring move 0.05 s earlier (4.9, 6.5); swatch bump 1 -> 1.1 -> 1 (0.2 s each way).
- **Shape morph** in sync with each reseed: clock squircle -> scallop (0.75 s `back.out(1.5)`) -> circle (0.7 s `back.out(1.4)`); deco scallop -> pill -> circle; weather widget radius 40 -> 131 (0.6 s).
- **Second container transform (8.0-8.65 s):** weather widget (264 x 258, radius 131) grows to the full 936 x 1140 screen, radius 131 -> 0, 0.65 s `power3.inOut`; its "24 / Nắng nhẹ" copy fades 0.2 s; card content fades 0.25 s from 8.4 s; title and subtitle y 20 -> 0 stagger 0.08; rows y 40 -> 0 `back.out(1.3)` stagger 0.07 from 8.5 s; preview tiles from 8.85 s.
- **Toggle flips** 9.2, 9.6, 10.0 s (0.4 s apart); each flips knob, pulses the matching preview tile 1 -> 1.06 and rotates the icon 45 deg (0.9 s `power3.out`). The last flip settles at 10.5 s, leaving 1.5 s of full, moving-but-complete composition.
- **Headline swap** 7.85 s: out 0.15 s (y -16, `power2.in`), in 8.0 s 0.4 s (y 24 -> 0). Max overlap of outgoing and incoming = 0.
- Holds always carry motion: screen scale drift, deco rotation, slider/knob springs.
- No shake, no flash, no whip, no elastic (energy 3: `back.out` only).

## 11. Scene recipes (12.0 s, 3 beats; the demo used these)

**R1 Personal screen (0-3.5 s).** Kicker + 2-line headline with hero word "màu" swelling; screen with five widgets springing in; slider thumb springs at 1.7 s. Focal point: the clock shape. Closes with the FAB press.
**R2 Seed picker (3.5-7.9 s).** FAB -> sheet container transform; ring and reseed to teal, then orange; all widgets retint and the clock morphs scallop -> circle while the sheet stays open; sheet collapses into the FAB (7.45-7.95 s).
**R3 Settings screen (7.9-12 s).** Headline swaps; weather widget expands to the full screen; three toggle rows flip; preview tiles pulse. Holds from 10.5 s to the end.

## 12. Must not

- Neon glows, hard shadows, borders on cards, sharp corners, grainy textures (profile).
- Colours that are not tones of the active seed (except the three swatch accents).
- `segoepr`; text under 30 px except one footer line; emoji.
- Real brand names, OS names, version numbers, prices: use generic content and "Minh hoạ".
- Terminal, code, spec scenes (profile avoid).
- Tweening more than one reseed at once.

## 13. Traps seen in real builds (this build, 2026-11-14)

1. **Container transform trips `inspect`.** A sheet or card that grows over live text gives `text_occluded` errors (12 on the first inspect) and `clipped_text` at the transform's own frames. Fix that worked: `data-layout-allow-occlusion` on the COVERED elements (clock, battery, music, chips), not on the cover; `data-layout-allow-overflow` on the cover and its content wrapper. Three `clipped_text` errors remain at t 3.7, 7.96 and 8.3 s (transient frames of the transform, the clipped content is at opacity 0 or still growing). Judged acceptable and visible in the render as a clean growth; they would need an inspect baseline to pass strict.
2. **Squircle corner overflow.** Superellipse n 4 with ax 250 gives radius 297 at 45 degrees, outside the 540 viewBox; the blob is cut. Use ax 220 (corner 261) or lower n.
3. **Segoe UI Variable has no usable 800.** The profile's "400 -> 800" swell was cut to 400 -> 700; 800 clamps (not measured on the axis table, only observed that the 700 render is the heaviest).
4. **Frame 0.3 s still fading.** With stagger 0.06 and `back.out`, chips and the music title are about 25-40% visible at 0.3 s (below 90% for roughly 0.6 s). It is an entrance, not a held frame, but the first 0.5 s is the weakest. Start the stagger at 0 or shorten the 0.55 s duration for the last items.
5. **Decorative blob under the second headline.** The 400 px blob at x 640 overlaps the "máy" of the wider second headline ("Mỗi chạm, cả máy", about 790 px at 100 px). Readable (ink on `pc` is above 15:1 in HAND math) but it is the only layered overlap; shorten the second headline or move the blob to x 700.
6. **Acc on pc is the tight pair (4.65:1, HAND).** Never put 32-46 px accent text on a primary container; it passes `validate` only because the accent text in this build is the 100 px hero on `bg`.
7. **Preview labels 32 px sit 8 px under their tiles.** The lower third is well used (content to y 1620) but the card bottom is 1140 high and the footer 70 px below: keep at least 40 px between the card and the footer or the footer reads as a card caption.
8. **Tooling in this sandbox.** `frame_check.py` needs numpy (not installed, pip install was refused), so the frame check was done by hand on an ffmpeg contact sheet (see selfcheck). `hyperframes inspect` has no `--describe` flag (the value `false` is read as the project dir). `snapshot --describe false` is accepted. Contrast numbers had to be computed by hand because ad-hoc scripts were not allowed to run; `validate` supplies the pass/fail.
9. **Morph needs no path files.** Interpolating four shape numbers is smoother and cheaper than pre-authored matching paths; 96 points were enough, no visible faceting at 540 px.
10. **Render cost.** `render -q standard` 12.0 s in 11.0 s wall; file 2.2 MB (flat fills compress extremely well).
